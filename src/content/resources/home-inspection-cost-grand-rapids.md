## Count the basement when measuring the house

Use the total reasonably available floor area of the primary building, including above-grade space and both finished and unfinished basement areas. A listing's advertised living area may not include all of that space.

Do not add garages, porches, decks, sheds, detached structures, or detached dwellings to the main-building number. Those are handled separately. If a listing, assessor record, and your own information disagree, identify the uncertainty before the inspection rather than guessing at a precise number.

## Condominiums and townhomes

A condominium inspection follows the contracted unit and accessible systems serving it, with ownership and access limitations made clear. It is not an inspection of every common building system or an association reserve study.

## Additional units and buildings

For a duplex, triplex, or four-unit property, the additional-unit adjustments are $100, $200, and $300 respectively, in addition to the applicable main-building price. The contracted units and relevant accessible common areas are included in the agreed scope; a sample of units is not a substitute for inspecting the contracted assignment.

A detached dwelling is priced separately: $250 through 1,000 square feet, with $40 for each additional 500-square-foot increment. It is not also charged as an additional unit inside the main building. Multiple buildings or unusual uses may require review.

An additional ordinary detached garage or simple outbuilding through 1,000 square feet is $75. A larger or powered workshop from 1,001–2,000 square feet starts at $125, subject to scope. An additional small shed with limited visual scope is $25. Complex buildings, hazardous uses, or commercial, industrial, or agricultural assignments require review and may be outside Rivermark's services.

## Travel and nonstandard assignments

Normal travel is included in Included territory. Extended travel is $75 total for one ordinary visit or $125 total for a normal two-visit assignment at one property; shared services do not stack travel charges. By-arrangement work needs an agreed travel quote before booking. West Olive, Macatawa and Ferrysburg are Extended; Spring Lake outside Ferrysburg remains Included. Physical-location exceptions apply even within an Included ZIP. Confirm the inspected property address and travel with Rivermark; a displayed scheduling quote may not include the correct charge. Use the [Service Area guidance](/service-area/) for address review.

Manual review is appropriate for:

- Homes over 6,000 square feet, more than four dwelling units, or unusual multi-building properties.
- Mixed uses, commercial or agricultural conditions, unusual systems, or complex detached dwellings.
- Restricted access, significant document review, outer-area travel, or an unreleased special schedule.
- Any assignment whose purpose or scope does not fit a published standard service.

Confirm that any optional service you need is available before relying on it in your plans. Each has its own scope and price.

## What does not create a routine extra charge

Rivermark's pricing policy does not add an automatic old-house surcharge, a routine card-processing fee, an extra fee for the ordinary PDF report, or an hourly overtime meter to a standard inspection. A routine deposit is not part of the standard policy.

A small difference between listing and measured area does not justify a surprise charge at the property. Materially inaccurate information or an expanded scope may require a revised price, explained before the additional work proceeds.

When the customer supplied materially accurate information and did not later expand the scope, Rivermark honors the accepted quote.

## Compare more than the advertised starting number

When comparing inspection prices, ask what the base allowance includes and what the final assignment requires:

- Does square footage include unfinished basement space?
- Is the ordinary detached garage included, and how are other buildings treated?
- Does the service cover the whole contracted property or only a limited consultation?
- How do ownership form, units, access, and optional services affect scope?
- What report, walkthrough, and report-related support are included?
- Are travel or ordinary payment-processing charges added later?

A limited pre-offer or investor consultation is not interchangeable with a full residential inspection. A low price for a different scope is not a like-for-like comparison.

## Measure this property

Appearance, age, foundation type, and nearby test results cannot establish this home's radon level. A separately contracted measurement records conditions at the actual property.

## From preparation to result

1. Confirm the property, service, timing, and lawful access for placement and retrieval with Rivermark.
2. Rivermark provides preparation instructions for building conditions, heating and cooling, doors and windows, occupants, pets, and device protection.
3. The inspector places the monitor in an appropriate location for the required measurement period.
4. Rivermark retrieves the monitor, reviews the data and conditions for validity or interference, and issues the written report.

Normal placement and retrieval are included. An add-on test can share scheduling and travel with a full inspection; a standalone test has separate access and travel needs. Neither transaction pressure nor the home-inspection timetable justifies shortening the measurement.

## Preparation, reporting, and limitations

The party arranging the service must provide lawful access and forward the preparation instructions to the appropriate owner, occupant, tenant, or manager. Maintain the required building conditions and protect the monitor from movement, covering, unplugging, or interference. Notify Rivermark if access or conditions change.

The written report records the measurement location and period, monitor information, deployment and retrieval, recorded conditions, result, validity review, limitations, and appropriate next-step guidance. It follows retrieval and data review, independently of the home-inspection report timetable.

Access problems, building-condition changes, device interference, power or equipment issues, or failure to follow instructions may invalidate or limit a result. Rivermark reviews the evidence before reporting a valid result, documenting a limitation, or requiring a repeat under the pricing policy above. A result describes the measurement period and does not guarantee future levels.

## Measurement, Not Mitigation

Rivermark does not:

- Perform radon mitigation
- Design or quote mitigation systems
- Guarantee future radon levels
- Diagnose individual health effects
- Represent a single result as permanent assurance
- Accept compensated mitigation referrals
- Require the customer to use a particular mitigation provider

When mitigation or additional evaluation is appropriate, the customer contracts directly with a qualified provider.

## Radon FAQs

### How does a radon test work?

Rivermark places a professional continuous monitor at the property, completes the required measurement period, retrieves the device, reviews the data and conditions for validity or interference, and issues a written report.

### How long does the test take?

Confirm the required measurement period and placement and retrieval timing with Rivermark. The applicable procedure and monitor requirements determine the timing.

Rivermark will not shorten the required process merely to satisfy transaction pressure.

### Can the test be completed during the home inspection?

The placement may be coordinated with the inspection when the timing, access, property conditions, equipment availability, and required measurement procedure support it.

The final radon result follows retrieval and validity review rather than the normal home-inspection report timetable.

### Can I order radon testing without a home inspection?

A standalone test is a separate service. Confirm current availability and whether the property, access, territory, timing, and equipment capacity fit.

### Where is the monitor placed?

Rivermark selects an appropriate location using the property conditions, applicable procedure, and device requirements.

### What conditions must be maintained?

The preparation instructions will explain the required building, access, heating and cooling, door and window, occupancy, pet, and device-protection conditions.

Failure to maintain them may invalidate or materially limit the measurement.

### What can interfere with a measurement?

Possible interference can include device movement or disconnection, failure to maintain required conditions, access disruption, equipment or power issues, and other circumstances affecting the procedure.

Rivermark reviews the data and recorded conditions before deciding whether the result is supportable.

### When will I receive the result?

After the required measurement period, retrieval, data review, and validity determination are complete.

### Does Rivermark perform mitigation?

No. Rivermark measures and reports radon. It does not design, quote, sell, or install mitigation systems.

### Does a low result guarantee future conditions?

No. The report describes the conditions recorded during the measurement period. It does not guarantee future levels.

### What happens if the measurement must be repeated?

A Rivermark-caused repeat is completed without charge.

A repeat caused by failure to maintain the required conditions or other customer/property-side interference may be charged according to the published policy.

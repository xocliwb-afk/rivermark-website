## Targeted Use During a Full Inspection

Rivermark may use a thermal camera during a full residential inspection when the inspector determines that it provides useful context for a particular condition.

That targeted use:

- Is included at the inspector's discretion
- Is not a promised systematic whole-home scan
- Does not guarantee that every area will be scanned
- Does not include a separate thermal deliverable
- Does not replace the visible inspection or appropriate specialist evaluation
- Does not establish a concealed cause from a thermal pattern alone

Customers seeking broader or purpose-specific thermal work should select an active paid service or request manual review.

## Questions Thermal Imaging May Help Investigate

Depending on the service, property, preparation, and environmental conditions, thermal imaging may provide useful context for:

- Atypical surface-temperature patterns near a suspected moisture concern
- Insulation or air-movement irregularities
- HVAC distribution differences
- Plumbing-risk areas
- Selected equipment surfaces
- Building-envelope questions
- Areas that deserve visible inspection, moisture-meter screening, or specialist evaluation

A thermal pattern may have more than one possible explanation. Rivermark describes what the available evidence supports rather than assigning an exact concealed cause without corroboration.

## Useful thermal imaging depends on the conditions present.

The service may be affected by:

- Indoor and outdoor temperature difference
- Weather and recent weather
- Solar loading
- Wind
- Heating or cooling operation
- Recent water use
- Surface materials and finishes
- Contents and stored belongings
- Access to the relevant areas
- Time of day
- Occupancy and normal building operation
- The purpose of the requested assessment

Rivermark may limit, convert, reschedule, postpone, or determine that the service is inconclusive when the conditions do not support useful imaging.

## Interpret patterns with supporting evidence

Rivermark compares the thermal pattern with visible conditions, materials, nearby systems, and environmental conditions. Non-invasive moisture or temperature measurements may help when practical and safe. The report describes what the evidence supports, using terms such as **thermal anomaly** when a specific cause cannot be established, and explains the appropriate next step.

## What the Customer Receives

The deliverable may include:

- Relevant thermal images
- Corresponding visible-light images
- Environmental and operating conditions
- Supporting visible observations
- Moisture-meter or other measurements when appropriate
- Controlled interpretation of what the evidence supports
- Material limitations
- Practical next-step guidance

The report includes the images that help explain the findings and supporting evidence; it does not include every image captured.

## Important Limitations

Thermal imaging does not provide:

- X-ray vision or the ability to see through walls
- Exact concealed-cause determination from a pattern alone
- Guaranteed moisture detection
- Guaranteed discovery of hidden defects
- Destructive or invasive investigation
- A complete home inspection unless separately contracted
- An energy-efficiency certification
- Exact R-values
- A blower-door test or measured air-leakage rate
- Electrical-safety certification
- Engineering or code certification
- Future-performance prediction
- Assurance that environmental conditions supported complete or conclusive imaging

The service reports surface-temperature patterns and related evidence under the conditions present at the time of the assessment.

## When to request individual review

Confirm service availability and whether the property, environment, schedule, and travel support useful imaging before an appointment is accepted.

Premium envelope work, large or unusual structures, narrow environmental timing, extensive analysis, outer territory, or a purpose that requires individual scoping should route to manual review.

## Thermal-Imaging FAQs

### Does a thermal camera see through walls?

No. It displays surface-temperature patterns.

Those patterns may support closer evaluation but do not reveal a complete concealed view or establish an exact cause by themselves.

### Is thermal imaging included in a full home inspection?

Targeted camera use may be included at the inspector's discretion when it provides useful context.

A systematic whole-home scan or separate thermal deliverable is not included unless a paid thermal service is contracted.

### Can thermal imaging find every leak?

No. Thermal imaging does not guarantee moisture detection or identify every leak.

A relevant pattern may require visible evidence, moisture-meter screening, operation of a system, improved access, or specialist evaluation.

### What conditions are needed?

Useful imaging may depend on temperature difference, weather, solar loading, wind, HVAC operation, recent water use, surface materials, access, contents, and timing.

The service may be limited, postponed, converted, or inconclusive when the conditions are not suitable.

### Is the service an energy audit?

No. The thermal services are not automatically a blower-door test, measured air-leakage assessment, exact R-value analysis, or complete energy audit.

### Can Rivermark diagnose the exact cause of a pattern?

Only when the available supporting evidence reasonably supports a specific conclusion.

Otherwise, Rivermark uses controlled language and recommends the appropriate next step.

### Is a standalone thermal service a substitute for a home inspection?

No. A standalone thermal assessment or screening is limited to the defined thermal purpose and does not replace the full residential inspection.

### Will I receive every thermal image?

No. Rivermark provides relevant images and supporting context rather than every image captured.

### Can electrical panels be scanned?

Electrical-panel imaging is conditional. It requires the appropriate service scope, inspector training, safety procedure, insurer and agreement coverage, safe access, naturally occurring load, and suitable property conditions. It is not a promise to open or scan every panel.

### What happens when conditions do not support useful imaging?

Rivermark may limit, convert, reschedule, postpone, or determine that the service is inconclusive rather than create an unsupported conclusion.

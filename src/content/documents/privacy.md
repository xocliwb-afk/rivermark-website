Rivermark Home Inspections respects the privacy of customers, prospective customers, real-estate professionals, website visitors, and other people who communicate with the company.

This Privacy Policy explains the types of information Rivermark may collect, how the information may be used, the categories of service providers that may receive it, and the choices available to you.

This policy applies to Rivermark's public website, adaptive Contact form and New Construction interest form, Price & Availability entry, inspection-management workflow, and related business communications. It does not replace the inspection agreement, report-use terms, payment terms, or another service-specific agreement.

## 1. Information You Provide

Rivermark may collect information you voluntarily provide, including:

- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Preferred communication method
- Property address
- Property type, size, age, ownership form, units, structures, occupancy, and access information
- Requested services
- Preferred or selected appointment information
- Client, agent, seller, occupant, association, builder, property-manager, or other authorized contact information
- Questions, messages, documents, and other information you submit
- Agreement, authorization, payment-status, and service information associated with an inspection order
- Feedback or review-related communication
- Accessibility requests
- New-construction interest information

Do not submit card numbers, security codes, government identifiers, portal passwords, or other highly sensitive information through the Contact form.

## 2. Inspection and Report Information

When you become a client, the inspection record may include:

- Inspection order and appointment information
- Signed agreements and addenda
- Payment and invoice status
- Property access and preparation details
- Inspection notes, photographs, videos, measurements, and observations
- Reports, corrections, revisions, and addenda
- Communications with the client and authorized participants
- Complaint, incident, or claim-related records when applicable

Inspection-specific records are handled through Rivermark's approved inspection-management and storage systems rather than being exposed through the public website.

## 3. Payment Information

Payment-card or bank information is entered into and processed by Rivermark's approved payment processor or inspection-platform payment service.

Rivermark's public website is not designed to store complete card numbers or security codes.

Rivermark may receive limited transaction information such as payment status, amount, date, invoice, refund, or chargeback information needed to administer the service and maintain financial records.

## 4. Information Collected Automatically

When you use the public website, Rivermark and its service providers may collect limited technical information such as:

- IP address
- Browser and device type
- Operating system
- Referring page or campaign
- Pages viewed
- Approximate visit time and interaction events
- Error, security, and server-log information
- Cookie or similar identifier information where used

No website analytics vendor is active. Website form information is not sent to analytics.

Rivermark may use information to:

- Respond to inquiries
- Qualify unusual properties or assignments
- Calculate or present pricing
- Schedule and administer inspections
- Create and maintain the official inspection order
- Obtain agreements and payment
- Coordinate access, preparation, reminders, deployment, retrieval, and appointments

- Correct reports or issue addenda
- Maintain records and backups

- Protect Rivermark, customers, property owners, and other people from fraud, misuse, or safety risk

## 6. How Information May Be Shared

Rivermark may share information with service providers and other parties only as reasonably needed for legitimate business purposes, including:

### Inspection-management platform

Price & Availability embeds Spectora Get a Quote and provides a link to continue directly in Spectora. Information entered there goes to Spectora; the website inquiry forms are a separate path.

Rivermark uses Spectora as the system for live inspection quoting, released availability, official inspection orders, agreements, payment state, client and agent records, the client portal, field workflow, reports, and inspection-specific communication.

### Hosting, security, and website operations

Website forms send the information entered to the Rivermark server. The server validates the submission and sends it through the Gmail API / Google Workspace to {{business-email}}. The website has no submission database and does not store form contents in browser storage. The Contact form asks for a topic; optional property details are submitted only for property or quote help. Changing topics retains entered values in page memory, but inactive property details are not sent. Temporary server memory supports delivery, rate limiting, and duplicate prevention. The application does not routinely log submission contents.

### Payment and accounting providers

The approved payment processor and accounting system may process transaction, invoice, refund, reconciliation, and tax-record information.

### Email, text, and communication providers

Google Workspace / Gmail processes and delivers website inquiries to the Rivermark business mailbox, where messages are handled and retained. A successful form response means Google accepted the message for delivery. Website inquiries do not create an inspection order or reserve an appointment. Website text-message delivery is not enabled.

### Analytics and search tools

The website does not use an analytics or webmaster integration. Direct customer and property identifiers are not sent to analytics.

### Professional advisers and insurers

Information may be shared with attorneys, accountants, insurance carriers, brokers, consultants, or other advisers when reasonably necessary.

### Legal, safety, and protection needs

Rivermark may disclose information when required by law, legal process, insurer obligations, or when reasonably necessary to protect safety, property, rights, records, or evidence.

### Business transfer

Information may be transferred as part of a merger, sale, financing, reorganization, or transfer of Rivermark, subject to applicable law and appropriate confidentiality protections.

Rivermark does not sell inspection findings to contractors and does not use inspection records to market or generate repair work.

## 7. Report Sharing and Authorized Participants

The contracting client controls ordinary report sharing, subject to the inspection agreement, report-use terms, and the applicable client-portal permissions.

An agent who refers or initiates scheduling does not automatically become the client or gain unrestricted report access.

Authorization to receive or view a report does not automatically create separate third-party reliance rights.

Rivermark may communicate an apparent urgent property or safety condition to an appropriate responsible party when prompt action is warranted.

## 8. Cookies and Similar Technologies

The website uses temporary in-memory controls to limit repeat form submissions. Embedded and linked services, including Spectora, may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies.

The website does not use optional analytics cookies. Changes to these practices will be reflected in this policy, with notice or choices where required.

## 9. Retention

Rivermark retains information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected and for legal, tax, insurance, contractual, security, recordkeeping, complaint, and claim needs.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods.

## 10. Security

Rivermark uses administrative, technical, and physical safeguards intended to protect information, including controlled accounts, multifactor authentication where available, restricted access, managed credentials, secure hosting, backups, and vendor oversight.

No website, email, cloud service, or storage system can guarantee absolute security.

Do not send highly sensitive information through an ordinary email or general website form.

## 11. Your Choices and Requests

You may:

- Opt out of nonessential text messages as instructed
- Unsubscribe from nonessential marketing email when offered
- Ask Rivermark to correct inaccurate contact information
- Ask questions about Rivermark's privacy practices
- Request reasonable access, deletion, or other action when applicable law and Rivermark's legal, insurance, contractual, and recordkeeping obligations permit it

A deletion request may not require Rivermark to remove records that must be retained for legitimate legal, insurance, tax, security, agreement, report-integrity, complaint, or claim purposes.

## 12. Children's Privacy

Rivermark's website and inspection services are not directed to children. Rivermark does not knowingly seek personal information directly from children through the public website.

## 13. Third-Party Websites and Services

The website may link to or embed third-party services, including Spectora, maps, payment, reports, or other tools.

Those providers may have their own privacy policies and security practices. Rivermark is responsible for selecting and configuring its providers but does not control every independent third-party practice.
